## Long description (App Store and Google Play)
(2125/4000)

```text
MineMind is a fan quiz for kids who love Minecraft and everything around it: mobs, blocks, crafting, biomes and survival. Short rounds, bright pixel art and questions that grow with you.

SOLO ROUNDS
• 8 questions per round with a timer
• Play by topic: Mobs and combat, Blocks and building, Crafting and smelting, Biomes and structures, Farming and animals, Villagers and enchanting, Nether, End and redstone, Survival basics
• Three levels: Builder, Explorer and Pro Mode
• Smart difficulty adjusts the questions to your recent answers
• "Fix your mistakes" brings back the questions you got wrong
• Hints: 50/50 and +10 seconds

1143 QUESTIONS IN THREE LANGUAGES
Multiple choice, true or false, picture questions and crafting-grid recipes. Every question is available in English, Ukrainian and Russian. The speaker button reads the question aloud.

PROGRESS AND REWARDS
• XP, levels and ranks from Wood to Netherite
• A daily quest and a day streak
• Achievements
• 24 collectible cards with Kubik, our pixel mascot, in different costumes

PLAY TOGETHER
• Private rooms: create a room, share the 6-character code and start when everyone is ready
• Big screen: a TV or projector shows the questions, and players answer on their phones. Great for a class or a family evening
• Classroom mode: play over the local Wi-Fi, one device hosts the match
• Challenge a friend with a link: the same 8 questions in the same order. Who scores more?

MADE WITH KIDS IN MIND
• No ads and no in-app purchases
• No chat and no free text between players
• No profile photos, no public profiles, no location
• Play anonymously: no name or email needed
• Nicknames come from safe word lists or pass a filter
• A parent check protects outside links, email, sharing and account deletion
• No third-party analytics or ad tracking
• Download or delete all your data in Settings

Cloud save is optional: a parent can link an email to keep progress and move it to another device.

All pixel art, sounds and music were made for MineMind.

Unofficial fan quiz, not affiliated with Mojang or Microsoft. Minecraft is a trademark of Mojang Synergies AB.
```
